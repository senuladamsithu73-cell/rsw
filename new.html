My Letter

Words From My Heart 📝

Everything I have ever wanted to tell you

You are not just my best friend; you are the best person I have ever met, Bunny.

Even though it has only been 32 weeks and 5 days (5,496 hours) of our friendship, you did things for me that a person cannot do even in 10 years.
You know I overthink a lot, and before I met you, I always thought about having a best friend like the ones in dramas and cartoons. But I met a friend—the bestest friend—who is infinitely better than I ever imagined.
The best memory I have is the June 18 memory. We enjoyed that day so much, and I will never forget it! 😂

You even sent me photos without hesitation, even though our friendship was not even one year old.
I know you don't like physical touch, but you didn't even say a single word when I touched you.

You always listened to my problems even when you had your own problems. You always thought about my happiness even when you were suffering inside because of your own troubles.
The only thing I feel like I gave you was a burden. 😭 I did things you were not expecting from me, and I became another reason for your heartbreak. I know I was the worst friend you have met.
Even your past friends never got scolded by me in that way, so I am really sorry. I know sorry is not enough and I cannot correct the mistakes I have done, but I promise it will not happen again. I promise I will never treat you that way again.
Even though I scolded you and did not understand your situation, I always thought from my side instead of your side.
I get jealous, not because I don’t trust you, but because I’m scared of losing you. I don’t like seeing you spend more time with others than with me.

I know you want me to change, and I’m trying, but sometimes I can’t control these jealous feelings. I just hope you understand that it comes from how much I value our bond, not from a lack of trust. 🤍
But you never gave up on me. You were always kind. You never said a single bad thing to me even when I was wrong. You apologized. I scolded you in a way that even your enemy would not, but the only thing you did was keep talking to me kindly and saying sorry.
You never changed your energy towards me. You always stayed the same person.

I know I cannot fix all the mistakes I made and the things I did that broke your heart. But from now on, I am not that person. I will not do anything that hurts you. I will rebuild the heart that I broke, and I will understand you.
I may feel like the worst person you have met, but thank you for choosing me as your best friend. Thank you for not giving up on me even when I was rude. Trust me, I will never do anything like that again.
Thank you for being the bestestestest friend. Thank you for bringing happiness into my life and not leaving me. I don't know how to thank you for staying. You treated me better than even my parents sometimes, and I will forever be grateful for you.
In every life, I need you. I don't know what the future holds, but I want us to be forever best friends with the same energy, same efforts, and no replacements.

Again, thank you for choosing me as your best friend even when you had many other friends. They would not treat you the way I badly treated you, so thank you is not enough. I am forever grateful for having you.
